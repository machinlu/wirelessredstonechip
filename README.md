### ✨ Key Features

* 💎 **Custom 3D Chip Models:** Features unique, high-quality 3D Transmitter & Receiver chips created with Blockbench.
* 🚀 **Standalone & Lightweight:** Built in **Kotlin** for PaperMC. No need for ItemsAdder or Oraxen!
* ⚡ **Seamless Resource Pack Pipeline:** Automatically serves the custom 3D model resource pack to players upon joining.
* 📦 **PaperMC Native & Optimized:** Built specifically for **PaperMC** utilizing PersistentDataContainer (PDC) for zero-lag transmission and zero ghost data.
* 🛠️ **Survival Friendly:** Clean crafting and configuration workflow designed for seamless gameplay.

---

### 🕹️ How to Use

1. **Craft or Obtain** your Wireless Redstone Transmitter & Receiver Chips.
2. **Set Frequency:** Rename your chip using an Anvil to pair Transmitters and Receivers to the same frequency.
3. **Place & Connect:** Place the Sender chip on your input power source and the Receiver chip on your redstone mechanism.
4. **Transmit!** Trigger the Sender, and watch the Receiver power your redstone machinery wirelessly across distances!
